Kingdom Strategy Planner App

How to open:
1. Unzip Kingdom_Strategy_Planner_App.zip.
2. Open the planner-app folder.
3. Double-click index.html.

This is an offline web app. It opens in Safari, Chrome, Edge, or another browser and stores notes locally on your device.

Your planning data is not sent to Beauty for Ashes or any outside system by this app.
